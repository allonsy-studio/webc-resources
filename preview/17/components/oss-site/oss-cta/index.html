oss-cta

OSS call to action, at the foot of the page. Locked: its copy and actions are fixed, and it takes no slots. The one setting is the heading level. Closes the page body, above the footer: the cta hook places it last. The Allons-y Studio name is signed in the signature face, Yellowtail, which oss-head declares. Its actions are norn-link-buttons, colored by setting the global accent tokens on oss-cta. The one place Allons-y Studio's contact address appears. The mark is inlined from the org's assets by scripts/inline-svg.js.

Props

Write them with the @ prefix, and :@ for an expression.

NameTypeDescription
@level number The heading level, to fit the page outline. Any other value gives the default. One of 2, 3, or 4. Falls back to 2.

Tokens
