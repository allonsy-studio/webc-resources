norn-rich-text

Prose styles for long-form content: headings, paragraphs, lists, quotes, tables, rules, and inline code, all at one line height. A heading that links to itself shows its # while it is hovered or has focus, where the pointer can hover and the page is at least 70rem wide. Elsewhere the # stays in line. Wrap the content in it: <norn-rich-text><template webc:nokeep @html="content"></template></norn-rich-text>.

<norn-rich-text> a container, from @allons-y/webc-patterns
