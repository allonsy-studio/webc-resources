norn-footer

Site footer: a row of columns, and a colophon below them. The default slot holds the columns: a lead <div> first (the name in a <p><strong>, a tagline, the license), then the links, then anything else, such as a credit. Put the links in one <nav aria-labelledby="…">, pointing at a hidden name: a <p><strong> heading and a <ul>, or for several groups a <div> each holding its heading and list. The groups sit side by side and wrap. When the footer is 60rem or wider, the lead column takes up to 25rem and the others size to their content; narrower, the columns wrap.
