norn-card

A card: a heading, then its text, on a raised surface with a colored band along the top. Pass the heading and body in the default slot, as in <norn-card><h3><a href="…">Title</a></h3><p>…</p></norn-card>. Pick the heading level for the outline: h3 under an h2. norn-card-grid sets the inherited --norn-card-rows to subgrid for its cards. It is not a setting: leave it alone elsewhere. In a norn-card-grid the first child, the second, and the third (a footer, such as a link) each take a row shared with the neighboring cards, so they line up. A card with a fourth child lays out on its own.
