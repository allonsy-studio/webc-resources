norn-card

A card: a heading, then its text, on a raised surface. A heading link makes the whole card a link, and a card with an accent has a 2px line along its top. Pass the heading and body in the default slot, as in <norn-card><h3><a href="…">Title</a></h3><p>…</p></norn-card>. Pick the heading level for the outline: h3 under an h2. A link in the heading is stretched over the card, so the card is one target. The pointer over the card takes a hover layer, and focus on the link draws the ring around the card. The link is not underlined until the pointer is over it. In a norn-card-grid the cards share their rows: the first child, the second, and the third (a footer, such as a link) each take a row shared with the neighboring cards, so they line up. A card with a fourth child lays out on its own.

Props

Write them with the @ prefix, and :@ for an expression.

NameTypeDescription
@accent string A 2px line along the top, in a global color: accent takes --norn-ui-accent, and info, success, warning, and danger take the matching --norn-status-* color. none draws no line.
@title-face string mono sets the heading in the mono face, as for package names.

Slots

NameDescription
default The heading, then the body.
